OUR STORY

A Taste of Ethiopia

Ahadu was created to celebrate the beauty of Ethiopian food and hospitality.

From the aroma of freshly roasted coffee to the traditional flavors of berbere, mitmita and Ethiopian butter, every dish tells a story.

Our guests come together around one table, share food, enjoy conversation and experience the warmth of Ethiopian culture.
